## Overview
The opening keynote of Microsoft Build 2026, led by figures like Satya Nadella, delves into the advancements and future directions of Microsoft's technology stack, emphasizing the AI ecosystem. The keynote aimed to encourage developers to harness these innovations to create meaningful value in their work, highlighting updates across compute technologies, AI models, developer tools, and enterprise software solutions. A significant focus was placed on integrating AI throughout Microsoft's product lineup, including Windows and various cloud and edge solutions.

## New Features & Announcements

- **Surface RTX, Dev VOX** — A high-performance developer machine featuring 1 Peta flop of AI compute, with 20 CPU cores and 128GB of unified memory, designed as a "dream machine" for developers. Availability expected in public preview.
  
- **Windows 365 Developer Enhancements** — Optimized for developer productivity in the cloud, featuring a distraction-free environment and native integration with GitHub Copilot.

- **Windows ML & Windows AI Expansion** — Improvement in local AI compute capabilities, allowing leveraging the existing base of GPUs on Windows.

- **Project Solara** — Introduction of agent-first devices for enterprise environments, with purpose-built form factors supporting a wide range of verticals and workflows.

- **Microsoft Execution Containers (MXC)** — New policy layer for Windows providing isolation and containment for processes, designed to support the execution of complex AI workloads.

- **Rayfin SDK** — Agent-first SDK to connect agents to backend services, allowing deployment integration with enterprise-managed environments.

## Topics Covered

- **AI and Infrastructure Integration** — Discussion of incorporating AI across Microsoft's infrastructure, from the edge with Windows devices to the cloud with comprehensive AI models, emphasizing the adaptability and extensibility of these systems to support more intelligent workloads.

- **Advancements in AI Models and Tools** — Introduction of new AI models across image, voice, transcription, and coding, each designed for specific tasks like reasoning and contextual understanding, optimized for real-world applications and scalability in enterprise environments.

- **Project Solara and Device Ecosystem** — Exploration of new devices designed for an agent-first world, meant to enhance productivity and connectivity across various work environments using cutting-edge AI-driven technologies.

- **Microsoft Discovery** — Highlighting the use of Microsoft Discovery in scientific research, providing tools and platforms for continuous AI-driven experimentation and discovery processes, exemplified by a case study in plastics recycling research.

- **Quantum Computing Advances** — Announcement of Majorana 2, a significant development in quantum computing, promising improved reliability and scalability of quantum computations, featuring much longer qubit lifetimes.

## Key Takeaways

- **AI as a Platform, Not Just a Tool** — Emphasis on maximizing the potential of AI across all platforms to create value beyond just technology itself.
- **Local AI Compute Expansion** — Continued focus on expanding local AI capabilities on Windows devices, providing more accessible and powerful tools for developers.
- **Enterprise Readiness and Security** — New developments focus heavily on ensuring enterprise readiness and enhanced security for AI applications, including MXC for containment.
- **Integration with Existing Workflows** — Tools like Windows ML and Project Solara highlight efforts to seamlessly integrate AI into existing business and development workflows.
- **Development Community Engagement** — Encouragement for the developer community to leverage new SDKs and collaborative platforms like Rayfin to build and refine their AI-driven applications.
- **Frontier Learning Approaches** — Introduction of systems like Frontier Tuning and RLEs designed to enhance and personalize learning processes for AI models across specific domains.

## Notable Quotes

- "It's not about any one piece of technology you hear about or even the platform itself; it is about the value you can build, you can compound, you can create on top of the platform." — Satya Nadella
- "For quantum to deliver real-world impact, it has to be both reliable and scalable."
