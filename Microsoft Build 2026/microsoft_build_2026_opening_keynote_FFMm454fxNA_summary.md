## Overview
The Microsoft Build 2026 Opening Keynote, presented by Satya Nadella and various guest speakers, covers the latest advancements in technology focusing on AI and its applications at edge and cloud levels. The conference explores the future landscape for developers amid significant tech shifts and outlines how to effectively participate in the emerging frontier intelligence ecosystem. Key takeaways include robust new AI stacks, the expansion of agent functionality, and how these technologies can transform computing and enterprise operations.

## New Features & Announcements
- **Surface RTX Spark DevBox** — A high-performance developer machine featuring 1 Peta flop of AI compute, 20 CPU cores, and 128GB of unified memory. Availability later this year.
- **Microsoft Execution Containers (MXC)** — New policy layer for containment and isolation of agents on Windows, enhancing security and compliance.
- **Web IQ** — A model-agnostic grounding service for fresh, high-quality, reliable web content in real-time.
- **Project Solara** — Introduction of new agent-first devices: stationary and portable devices geared for enterprise, leveraging agent-driven interaction models.
- **MAI Models Announcement** — Release of seven new models across various domains, including image, voice, and coding, all focused on efficiency and accuracy.
- **Open Claw Integration** — Open Claw now available on Windows with security sandboxing using the MXC policy layer.

## Topics Covered
- **AI Stack Innovations**: The keynote discusses Microsoft’s AI stack which includes ubiquitous compute fabric, models, tools, runtime, and deployment at both the edge and cloud levels. The stack serves as the foundation for AI application development, with a focus on developer empowerment.
  
- **Edge and Cloud Developments**: Innovations in edge computing, with emphasis on Windows ML and AI capabilities on local devices to leverage the vast compute power of NPUs, GPUs, and CPUs. Expansion of cloud capabilities to support heterogeneous workloads was also discussed, including more efficient and reliable data center designs.

- **Agent-First Computing**: Introduction to Project Solara and the shift towards computing systems centered around agent-driven interactions. Discussion on Windows being repositioned as a platform for one-stop agent operation.

- **Security and Compliance with AI**: Microsoft introduces enhanced security measures for AI applications, such as Microsoft Execution Containers (MXC) for ensuring agent actions are isolated and compliant.

- **New Developer Tools**: GitHub Copilot app is presented as a comprehensive tool for managing coding sessions and fostering AI collaborations efficiently. Additionally, Rayfin provides backend services for enterprise deployment.

- **Frontiers in Scientific Discovery**: Microsoft Discovery aims to revolutionize scientific research using AI-driven processes that support continuous, automated experimentation and discovery.

- **Advancements in Quantum Computing**: Announcement of Majorana 2 with significantly improved qubit lifetimes, advancing the path toward scalable and reliable quantum computing.

## Key Takeaways
- AI agents are becoming central to computing, with advancements enabling them to work autonomously across various platforms and environments.
- Enhanced computing capabilities at the edge and cloud level will facilitate powerful AI applications with improved security, efficiency, and reach.
- New hardware and software innovations such as Surface RTX Spark DevBox and Project Solara devices highlight the next-gen developer tools and experiences.
- Microsoft's commitment to open ecosystems ensures various AI models and tools are accessible across the community and industry partners.
- Innovations such as Web IQ and Agent 365 SDK highlight the importance of integrating real-time intelligence and governance in enterprise-grade AI solutions.
- Security enhancements such as Microsoft Execution Containers underscore the importance of secure, compliant AI deployments.
- The keynote underscores the role of collaboration, co-creation, and shared platforms in driving the next wave of technological advancements, particularly in AI and quantum computing.

## Notable Quotes
- "The question is not about whether you can build the next great model or platform even this quantum machine. It is the question of how do we build the frontier ecosystem together." — Satya Nadella
- "Agents change the whole nature of the device in itself. Starting with the fact that if AI understands the world as we understand it, it will be closer to our senses." — Cristiano Amon, Qualcomm
- "The next computer is all of the devices working together as one system with agents closing up closer to where and when you need them." — Stevie Bathiche, Microsoft
- "With Project Solara, it's about creating a system that extends your agent across a constellation of devices." — Stevie Bathiche, Microsoft
- "You're no longer just a consumer of frontier models, but you're now a full participant." — Satya Nadella
