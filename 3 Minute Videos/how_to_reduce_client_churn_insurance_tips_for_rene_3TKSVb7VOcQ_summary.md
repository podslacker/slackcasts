## Overview  
The video offers practical strategies for insurance agencies to reduce client churn and turn renewals into growth opportunities. It emphasizes proactive communication, handling cancellations thoughtfully, and training reps to become “retention rock stars.” The presenter also highlights resources like agency school, on‑demand videos, and live virtual training to help agencies implement these tactics.

## Topics Covered  
- **Renewal as a relationship checkpoint** – Treat renewals as a chance to reinforce value, uncover gaps, and cross‑sell, rather than a simple price discussion.  
- **Proactive renewal review calls** – Reach out 45‑60 days before a policy expires, discuss market conditions, identify coverage gaps, and pitch multi‑line solutions to increase client longevity.  
- **Managing cancellations proactively** – Listen for the underlying reason (price, coverage confusion, life change), offer adjustments or payment options, and ensure the conversation ends on a positive note to preserve referrals and future re‑engagement.  
- **Building a retention‑focused team** – Use role‑playing, script libraries, and side‑by‑side coaching to teach reps how to handle tough renewal conversations with confidence.  
- **Training resources** – Agency School’s on‑demand videos and live virtual classrooms are available to help agents master retention skills and customize processes for their agency.  
- **Call to action** – Visit the website, explore blogs, videos, and tools, and connect on social media for ongoing support.

## Key Takeaways  
- Most client loss stems from silence and missed renewal strategy, not price.  
- Initiate renewal review calls 45‑60 days early to shift the dialogue from price to risk management and cross‑selling.  
- Turn cancellation calls into discovery calls—understand the reason before processing any termination.  
- Multi‑line clients have higher retention rates; aim to convert monoline accounts through proactive reviews.  
- Retention is a trainable skill; equip reps with scripts, role‑plays, and mentorship from top performers.  
- Leverage agency school and virtual training to embed a retention‑first culture across the team.  

## Notable Quotes

- “When our reps believe they can save accounts, they most certainly do.”
