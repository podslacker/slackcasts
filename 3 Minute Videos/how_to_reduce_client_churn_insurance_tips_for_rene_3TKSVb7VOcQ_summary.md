## Overview
In this episode, the host shares actionable strategies for insurance agencies to reduce client churn and turn renewals into growth opportunities. The focus is on proactive renewal calls, handling cancellations thoughtfully, and training reps to become “retention rock stars.” Listeners are encouraged to tap into the host’s resources—training videos, live workshops, and a supportive community—to improve their agency’s retention processes.

## Topics Covered
- **Why churn happens** – Clients typically leave due to silence, missed opportunities, and lack of a renewal strategy, not price alone.  
- **Proactive renewal review calls** – Reach out 45‑62 days before a policy expires, discuss market conditions, spot coverage gaps, and position cross‑selling as risk‑management, not just a price talk.  
- **Handling cancellations proactively** – Treat every cancellation call as a conversation: ask why, uncover pricing, coverage confusion, or life‑change issues, and offer adjustments or payment options before processing the cancel.  
- **Training reps for retention** – Retention is a teachable skill; use role‑plays, side‑by‑side listening, scripts, and structured objection handling to build confidence.  
- **Resources offered** – On‑demand Agency School videos, live virtual training sessions, a community of reps, and a library of blogs and YouTube videos for continual learning.  
- **Mindset shift** – Move from reactive to leading the renewal conversation; a well‑handled churn—even if the client leaves—can still generate referrals and future wins.

## Key Takeaways
- **Start renewal conversations early** (45‑62 days out) to turn potential churn into multi‑line growth.  
- **Use renewal reviews to educate** clients on market trends and uncover hidden coverage needs.  
- **Treat every cancellation as a diagnostic call**; address price, understanding, or life‑change concerns before confirming the loss.  
- **Invest in retention training**—role‑playing, scripts, and mentorship turn reps into proactive retainers.  
- **Multi‑line clients stay longer**; aim to move monoline accounts into broader coverage relationships.  
- **Your handling of churn impacts referrals**—a respectful exit can lead to future business.  
- **Leverage the host’s training ecosystem** (videos, live classes, community) to embed these practices agency‑wide.

## Notable Quotes
- “Retention isn’t about reacting. It’s about **leading the renewal conversation**.”  
- “Even if they still leave, **how we handle it impacts our referrals and future wins**.”  
- “Retention rock stars can be **trained and learned**—it’s not just luck.”
