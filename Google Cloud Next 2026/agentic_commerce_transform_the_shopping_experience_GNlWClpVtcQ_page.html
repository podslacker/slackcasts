D. Open, Vendor‑Agnostic Standards

Protocol Purpose Core Features
UCP – Universal Commerce Protocol Standard schema for AI‑to‑backend commerce interactions. • Catalog, Cart, Checkout, Order, Identity Linking
• Extensible (discounts, fulfillment as “decorators”)
• Transport‑agnostic (JSON/REST, MCP, A2A, iframe)
AP2 – Agent Payments Protocol Secure, tamper‑proof payment flow for agents. • Tokenized checkout
• Mandates guaranteeing price & intent
• Works with existing digital wallets & PSPs

Both protocols are open‑source (ucp.dev, GitHub) and designed to avoid vendor lock‑in.

E. Architecture Snapshot (UCP)

[Consumer Surface] → Platform (Agent/App)
      ↓
[UCP] – Service Layer (Shopping, Cart, Checkout, …)
      ↓
[Common Layer] – Identity Linking, Security
      ↓
[Capabilities] – Catalog, Order, … + Extensions (Discounts, Fulfillment)
      ↓
[Transport] – REST/JSON, MCP, A2A, iframe
      ↓
[Backend Actors] → Merchant of Record, Credential Provider, Payment Service Provider

The protocol mediates four actors (Platform, Merchant, Credential Provider, PSP) while keeping the merchant as the seller of record.

F. Payments & Trust (AP2)
