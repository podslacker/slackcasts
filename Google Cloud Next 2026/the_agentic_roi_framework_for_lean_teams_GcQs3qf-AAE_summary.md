# The agentic ROI framework for lean teams

## Overview  
The session introduces the **Agentic ROI framework** as a practical way for lean teams to turn AI hype into measurable business value.  Through a mix of theory, a real‑world case study from One United Bank, and a live demo of AI agents managing cloud spend, the presenters show how to identify high‑friction tasks, automate them with AI agents, and then use the freed‑up capacity for strategic product development.  

## Topics Covered  
- **The value‑realization problem** – Companies often pour billions into AI without first proving ROI, leading to projects that stall after six‑to‑twelve months.  
- **Three‑phase framework**  
  1. **Identify friction** – Pinpoint repetitive, low‑complexity tasks (e.g., account opening, loan processing, password resets).  
  2. **Automation with agents** – Deploy AI agents to handle those tasks, buying back employee capacity.  
  3. **Transformation** – Reallocate saved capacity to building differentiated, competitive products.  
- **One United Bank case study**  
  - Rapid customer‑base growth in 2020 created a surge of voice‑channel support requests.  
  - Legacy phone tree could not scale; the bank introduced intent‑based routing using Dialogflow, turning free‑form speech into actionable intents.  
  - Automation of authentication and knowledge‑base retrieval gave agents a 360° view before the call, reducing handling time.  
  - Result: training time cut from weeks to days, lower‑skill staff could handle routine queries, and overall call‑center efficiency improved dramatically.  
- **Extending the success** – The same AI stack was reused to combat “friendly fraud” disputes, integrating real‑time APIs and Salesforce case management for faster, more accurate resolutions.  
- **Demo preview** – An “army of AI agents” that continuously monitors cloud spend, generates reports, forecasts usage, and recommends optimizations—illustrating how the framework applies beyond contact‑center use cases.  

## Key Takeaways  
- **Start with ROI**: Quantify the business outcome before writing any code; otherwise projects risk becoming sunk cost.  
- **Focus on high‑volume, low‑complexity work** to achieve quick wins and measurable impact.  
- **AI agents can replace manual steps** (authentication, routing, knowledge retrieval) and free up skilled staff for higher‑value activities.  
- **Intent‑based routing turns unstructured voice input into actionable data**, improving both customer experience and internal analytics.  
- **Reusing the same AI components across different problems** (e.g., support and fraud) multiplies ROI and accelerates time‑to‑value.  
- **Lean teams can compete with larger rivals** by leveraging AI to automate at scale rather than adding headcount.  

## Notable Quotes  
- “The hardest thing is not building the technology; it’s **asking the business‑value question before the first line of code is written**.”  
- “We cut training from **weeks to days** by focusing on **one intent, one purpose** per low‑skill agent.”  
- “When you **buy back capacity** with automation, you can invest that time into building products that **actually differentiate you from the competition**.”
