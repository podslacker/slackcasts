# The agentic ROI framework for lean teams

**Video ID:** `GcQs3qf-AAE`

## Overview  
The video introduces the **Agentic ROI Framework**, a structured approach for lean teams to turn AI initiatives into measurable business value. Patik (Google Cloud Ops) and Andre (Google Financial Services) walk through a value‑realization model, illustrate it with a real‑world case study from One United Bank, and demo an AI‑agent solution for cloud‑spend management. The session emphasizes moving from AI hype to hard ROI, automating repetitive work, and using the freed capacity for differentiated product development.

## Topics Covered  

- **Why ROI matters for AI projects** – Companies often invest billions in AI without first quantifying business outcomes, leading to “failed” pilots once the hype fades.  
- **Three‑phase Agentic ROI Framework**  
  1. **Identify friction** – Spot high‑volume, low‑complexity manual tasks (e.g., account opening, loan processing, password resets).  
  2. **Automation with agents** – Deploy AI agents to handle those tasks, buying back employee capacity.  
  3. **Transformation** – Redirect the reclaimed capacity toward building competitive products and services.  
- **One United Bank case study**  
  - Rapid customer‑base growth (doubling in 60 days) strained a lean call‑center.  
  - Implemented intent‑based routing and natural‑language processing via Google Dialogflow to replace a cumbersome phone‑tree.  
  - Resulted in faster authentication, richer customer context for agents, and the ability to handle routine calls with less‑skilled staff.  
- **Key performance impacts**  
  - Training time reduced from weeks to days.  
  - Call‑handling efficiency improved; agents spent less time on repetitive tasks.  
  - Enabled fraud‑dispute automation by re‑using authentication flows and integrating with external dispute APIs.  
- **Demo preview** – An “army of AI agents” that continuously monitors, reports, forecasts, and optimizes cloud spend across an organization, illustrating the framework in a cloud‑cost‑management context.  

## Key Takeaways  

- **Start with business value**: Define the ROI question before writing any code.  
- **Target high‑volume, low‑complexity work** to gain quick wins and demonstrate impact.  
- **AI agents can replace or augment human labor**, allowing lean teams to do more with fewer people.  
- **Data captured during automation feeds subsequent use cases**, creating a virtuous cycle of improvement (e.g., moving from call routing to fraud‑dispute handling).  
- **Transformational impact comes after automation**, when the saved capacity is redirected to strategic product work.  
- **Real‑world proof**: One United Bank achieved faster onboarding, reduced training overhead, and expanded fraud‑handling capabilities without adding headcount.  

## Notable Quotes  

- “It wasn’t that the technology failed; it was that no one asked the business‑value question before the first line of code was written.”  
- “Every dollar that is spent is tied to business outcome… you have to answer the question ‘where’s the ROI?’”  
- “We took training from multiple weeks down to days by focusing on one intent, one purpose.”  
- “We’re not throwing money or people at a problem; we’re using AI because it’s a necessity to meet the surge.”
