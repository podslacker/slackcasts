Google Cloud Next – Gemini Enterprise Announcement

Speaker(s): Thomas Kurian (CEO, Google Cloud) & Sundar Pichai (CEO, Google)
Event: Google Cloud Next 2024 (Keynote)
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11PBno-cJ1g

1️⃣ Overview

The keynote unveils Gemini Enterprise, Google’s end‑to‑end “agentic” platform that turns AI models into production‑ready, secure, and governed digital agents for every employee and workflow.

  • Why now?

    • 75 % of Google Cloud customers already use Google AI in production.
    • The shift is from pilots to enterprise‑wide deployment.
    • Success hinges on a unified stack – chips, models, data, agents, and security all tightly integrated.

  • Core promise: A single, secure “mission‑control” environment where data, business logic, and AI models converge to deliver autonomous, value‑adding action at scale.
