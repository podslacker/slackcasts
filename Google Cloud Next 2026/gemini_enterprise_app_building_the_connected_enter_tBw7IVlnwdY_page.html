Overview

The video introduces Gemini Enterprise, a unified AI platform designed to turn the growing wave of enterprise agents into a cohesive, secure, and context‑aware work environment. Hosted by product leaders Demetri, Sammy, and guest Florian from Signal Iduna, the session walks through the three foundational pillars of the solution—creation, agent execution, and deep contextual integration—and demonstrates how these elements together solve fragmentation, tool fatigue, and governance challenges for modern organizations.

New Features & Announcements

  • Canvas (Interactive Creation Space) – An in‑app, side‑by‑side editor that auto‑launches from chat, pulls context, connectors, and sources to generate drafts of documents or slide decks, with native Google Workspace embedding or a robust proprietary editor.
    Availability: Public Preview

  • Integrated Agent Designer – A low‑code, natural‑language interface with a template gallery that lets anyone build, test, and iterate on sophisticated agents, supporting conditional logic, loops, human‑in‑the‑loop checks, and debugging tools.
    Availability: Preview (EAP coming soon)

  • Gemini Enterprise Projects – Persistent, bounded workspaces that act as a shared “brain” for teams, preserving context, versioned files, and enabling co‑creation chats where AI and humans iterate on assets together.
    Availability: Available now in preview

Topics Covered

  • Market reality & pain points – Rapid rise in agent adoption (35 % employee usage) creates silos, tool overload, budget opacity, and heightened security/governance risk.
  • Fabric of universal intelligence – The overarching framework that makes Gemini Enterprise the single front‑door, adapting to existing workflows while ensuring compliance.
  • Pillar 1 – Ask & Create Anything
    • Utilises Gemini 3.1 Pro & Flash models for complex reasoning.
    • Provides generative media (Nano Banana 2 for images, Veo 3 for video).
    • Introduces Canvas for automatic document/slide generation, with seamless Google Workspace integration or native editors that include AI‑driven tone/length sliders, versioning, and one‑click export.
  • Pillar 2 – Deploy Agents to Get Work Done
    • Central registry for native, pre‑built, third‑party, and custom agents.
    • One‑click company‑wide rollout with built‑in IAM, policies, and gateways.
    • New agent designer supports both agentic (creative) and deterministic (controlled) orchestration, human‑in‑the‑loop checks, iterative feedback loops, and playback debugging.
  • Pillar 3 – Harness Deep Integrated Context
    • Continuous access to enterprise data sources (Drive, Gmail, Outlook, OneDrive, etc.).
    • Ability to store “saved memories” such as brand voice or formatting preferences.
    • Projects provide persistent, bounded contexts that keep files and chats together, enabling AI‑generated overviews for new stakeholders and eliminating repetitive uploads.
  • Co‑creation & Team Collaboration
    • Shared group chat inside a Project where humans and AI co‑author assets in real time, preserving alignment and reducing back‑and‑forth across tools.
  • Enterprise Governance & Security
    • Centralized admin console with audit logs, model‑armor protection (prompt‑injection mitigation, PII masking, harmful‑content filtering).
    • Leverages existing IAM roles for least‑privilege access controls.
  • Customer case study – Signal Iduna
    • Overview of a large German insurer adopting Gemini Enterprise (renamed “Cozy” internally) to modernize AI use while maintaining strict regulatory governance and fostering AI literacy across the organization.
