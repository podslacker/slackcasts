Overview

The video introduces Gemini Enterprise, a unified AI platform designed to eliminate tool‑fatigue and fragmentation in modern workplaces. Demetri, Sammy, and Florian walk through the three core pillars of the product—creation, agent execution, and deep contextual integration—showing how the app acts as a single “front door” for enterprise AI while meeting strict compliance and governance requirements. Real‑world usage at Signal Iduna illustrates how the platform can be deployed at scale in a highly regulated industry.

New Features & Announcements

  • Canvas (interactive creation space) – An AI‑driven workspace that automatically launches beside chat, pulls context, connectors, and sources to generate first drafts of documents or slide decks, with native editors for Google Workspace or a built‑in HTML editor.
    Availability: Public preview

  • Integrated Agent Designer – A low‑code, natural‑language interface with a template gallery that lets anyone build, test, and iterate agents; supports conditional loops, agentic & deterministic orchestration, and human‑in‑the‑loop checks.
    Availability: Preview (EAP coming soon)

  • Gemini Enterprise Projects – Persistent, bounded workspaces that serve as a shared knowledge hub; AI output is scoped to files and chats uploaded to the project, enabling team‑wide collaboration and AI co‑authoring.
    Availability: Currently in preview
