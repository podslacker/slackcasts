1️⃣ Overview

The session tackles a common executive dilemma: how to ship products faster, globally, and with a unique competitive edge – without simply adding headcount.
The answer presented is the strategic use of AI‑powered agents.

The agenda:

  1. Value Realization Framework – moving from AI hype to hard ROI.
  2. Case study – James explains how One United Bank applied AI since 2020.
  3. Demo – an “army of AI agents” that autonomously manage cloud spend (reporting, optimization, forecasting, allocation).

2️⃣ Major Sections & Core Topics

Section Key Points
Intro & The Problem • Boardroom pressure → massive AI investment, but ROI often disappears after 6‑12 mo.
• Lean teams can’t afford “science projects” without clear business value.
Value Realization Framework Three‑phase model:
1. Identify Friction – locate high‑volume, low‑complexity manual tasks (e.g., account opening, loan processing, password resets).
2. Automation – deploy AI agents to automate those tasks, buying back capacity.
3. Transformation – freed resources can now focus on building differentiated products.
One United Bank – Real‑World Application • Mission‑driven digital bank with ~150 staff.
• 2020 surge: customer base doubled in 60 days (COVID‑19, social unrest).
• Friction: outdated 7‑layer phone tree; callers stuck on voice channel.
• Solution: AI‑driven natural‑language routing using Dialogflow → intent‑based routing & automatic authentication.
Results & KPIs • Call‑center efficiency – reduced training from weeks to days; less skilled staff handling routine intents.
• Automation gains – screen‑pop of 360° customer view, knowledge‑base answers delivered in‑call.
• Scalability – handled doubled call volume without additional headcount.
Extending the Success • Leveraged same AI platform for fraud‑dispute handling (authentication, case linking in Salesforce, real‑time dispute API).
• Demonstrates how one successful AI deployment can seed multiple downstream use cases.

3️⃣ Key Takeaways

Takeaway Why It Matters
Start with business pain, not technology hype ROI is realized only when the chosen AI solves a real, measurable friction point.
Three‑phase framework delivers repeatable value Identifying friction → automating → transforming creates a virtuous cycle and justifies further investment.
AI agents can replace or augment human effort cost‑effectively Even with a lean team, intent‑based routing and automated authentication cut training time dramatically.
Data collected from the first AI win fuels additional use cases One implementation (call routing) generated the knowledge base and authentication flow later reused for fraud dispute handling.

5️⃣ Action Checklist

  1. Map friction points – list high‑volume, low‑complexity processes.
  2. Select an AI platform (e.g., Google Dialogflow) and prototype a single use case.
  3. Define KPIs (time‑to‑train, call‑handle time, headcount saved, cost per transaction).
  4. Iterate & expand – reuse data, models, and integration layers for new processes (e.g., fraud, compliance).
  5. Report ROI to executives using the three‑phase framework to secure ongoing investment.

JORDAN: So the key takeaway is that AI agents aren’t just a shiny toy; they’re a systematic approach to capture value at each stage of the customer journey.
